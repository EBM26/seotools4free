How to make an ico file?

Making an ICO file, which is the traditional format used for website favicons, is fairly simple these days thanks to numerous free online tools designed specifically for this purpose. The easiest method is to start with a square image, ideally at least 256x256 pixels for the best quality across different display sizes, then upload it to a free favicon generator website like favicon.io, RealFaviconGenerator, or ConvertICO, which will automatically convert your image into a properly formatted ICO file, often bundling multiple icon sizes within that single file so browsers can choose the most appropriate resolution depending on where it's being displayed. If you prefer working with design software, programs like Adobe Photoshop, GIMP, which is free, or even certain online design tools like Canva can export images in ICO format directly, or you can save your design as a PNG first and then convert it separately using one of the online converter tools mentioned earlier. When designing your source image before conversion, keep in mind that favicons display extremely small, so simple, bold shapes with high contrast tend to translate much better than complex, detailed designs that would only be legible at larger sizes. Once you have your ICO file, you'll upload it to your website's root directory or through your CMS's settings.
