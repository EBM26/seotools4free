how to prompt ai to write like a human

To get AI to write more like a human, the key is giving it detailed stylistic direction rather than just a topic to cover. Start by specifying tone explicitly, like conversational, casual, warm, or direct, since AI defaults to a fairly neutral, formal register unless told otherwise. Ask it to vary sentence length, mixing short punchy sentences with longer flowing ones, since AI tends to produce uniformly structured sentences that feel robotic when left unchecked. Instructing the AI to use contractions like 'don't' and 'it's,' include occasional rhetorical questions, and avoid overly formal transition words like 'furthermore' or 'moreover' also helps a lot. Providing a sample of your own writing or a specific author's style as a reference gives the AI something concrete to mimic, which works far better than abstract instructions alone. You can also ask it to avoid common AI patterns, like starting every paragraph the same way or over-explaining obvious points. Finally, reviewing and editing the output afterward, adding your own personal anecdotes, opinions, or quirks, is often what truly makes the difference between AI-assisted writing and something that reads as genuinely human.
