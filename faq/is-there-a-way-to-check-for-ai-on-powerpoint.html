is there a way to check for ai on powerpoint?

There isn't a built-in AI detection feature inside PowerPoint itself, but you can check whether a PowerPoint's text content was likely AI-generated by running the text through a dedicated AI detection tool. The typical process involves copying the text content out of the slides, since most AI detectors are built to analyze text rather than slide files directly, and pasting it into a detector to get a probability score. Some AI detection services do support direct document uploads, including PowerPoint files, which can save you the step of manually extracting the text. It's worth keeping in mind that AI detectors analyze writing patterns and are not perfectly reliable, they can produce false positives on human-written text and false negatives on AI-generated text that's been edited. If you're specifically trying to detect AI-generated images or design elements within a presentation, rather than text, that's a separate and generally less reliable process, since dedicated tools for detecting AI-generated visuals are less mature than text detectors.
