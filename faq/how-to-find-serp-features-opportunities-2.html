How to find serp features opportunities?

Finding SERP feature opportunities involves identifying search queries relevant to your business where Google is already displaying enhanced result types, like featured snippets, People Also Ask boxes, local packs, image carousels, or video results, since these represent chances to capture extra visibility beyond a standard blue link listing. Start by using SEO tools like Semrush, Ahrefs, or Moz, which have dedicated SERP feature reports that show you exactly which of your target keywords currently trigger specific SERP features and whether you're already ranking for them or a competitor is. Pay particular attention to featured snippets, since these often appear above the first organic result, and you can identify opportunities by looking for keywords where a competitor holds the snippet with content that's thin, outdated, or poorly structured, giving you a chance to create more thorough content structured specifically to answer that query clearly and concisely. For People Also Ask opportunities, look at the actual questions appearing for your target keywords and create content that directly and clearly answers those specific questions using clear headers. Local businesses should also focus heavily on optimizing for local pack results through Google Business Profile optimization and consistent local citations.
