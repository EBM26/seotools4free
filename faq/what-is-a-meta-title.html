What is a meta title?

A meta title, often called a title tag, is the clickable headline that appears at the top of a search engine results listing, and it's also the text that typically shows up in a browser tab when someone has your page open. It's defined in the HTML head section of a webpage using the title tag, and while it might seem like a small detail, it's actually one of the most important on-page SEO elements because it directly influences both search rankings and click-through rates from the search results page. A well-crafted meta title should clearly and accurately describe what the page is about, ideally include your primary target keyword naturally near the beginning, and stay within roughly 50 to 60 characters to avoid getting cut off with an ellipsis in search results, since Google displays titles based on pixel width rather than a strict character count. Beyond SEO benefits, a compelling meta title also plays a psychological role in convincing searchers to actually click your result over competing listings, since it's often the very first impression someone has of your content before they even visit your site. Each page on your website should have a unique, descriptive meta title tailored specifically to that page's content.
