What colors are ada compliant?

No color is automatically ADA compliant in every combination. Compliance depends mainly on contrast between foreground and background: normal text generally needs stronger contrast than large text, and information should not be communicated by color alone. The exact steps vary by platform, theme, browser, and account permissions, so verify the current interface before making a production change. Make a backup or record the previous setting, test on a staging site when possible, and inspect the result on both desktop and mobile. A change that looks correct in an editor may be altered by caching, templates, redirects, or a content delivery network. If the result affects tracking, accessibility, or search visibility, test with the relevant browser tools and analytics reports rather than relying only on appearance. For a real project, the best final check is to compare the result with the original goal, test a few realistic cases, and revise anything that feels forced or misleading. A clear implementation is usually more durable than an elaborate one.
