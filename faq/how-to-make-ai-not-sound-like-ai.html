How to make ai not sound like ai?

Making AI-generated writing sound less like AI comes down to actively countering the patterns that make AI text recognizable in the first place. Start by asking for varied sentence structure and length, since AI naturally tends toward uniform, evenly paced sentences that feel repetitive over longer passages. Request contractions, casual phrasing, and a specific tone, like witty, blunt, or warm, rather than leaving the tone open-ended, since AI defaults to a neutral, slightly formal register unless directed otherwise. Explicitly tell it to avoid common AI tells, like overusing words such as 'delve,' 'moreover,' or 'in today's world,' and to skip the tendency to summarize everything at the end of a response, which reads as unnecessarily repetitive to human readers. Providing real examples of writing you like, whether your own past work or a specific style you admire, gives the AI a concrete target to aim for instead of guessing at vague instructions. It also helps to break up generation into smaller sections and edit as you go rather than generating one long piece all at once, since quality tends to degrade over longer outputs. Ultimately, combining specific prompting techniques with genuine human editing afterward produces the most convincing, natural-sounding results.
