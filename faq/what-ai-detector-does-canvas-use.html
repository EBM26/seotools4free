What ai detector does canvas use?

Canvas, the popular Learning Management System (LMS) developed by Instructure, does not have a single built-in native AI detector of its own. Instead, Canvas integrates with third-party anti-plagiarism and AI detection platforms chosen and licensed by individual schools, colleges, and universities. The most common AI detector integrated into Canvas is Turnitin, which includes an AI writing detection module designed to flag ChatGPT and other language model outputs directly within the Canvas SpeedGrader tool. Other educational institutions integrate alternative third-party tools into Canvas via LTI (Learning Tools Interoperability) standards, such as Copyleaks, Unicheck, PlagScan, or Winston AI. Therefore, the specific AI detector scanning student assignments on Canvas depends entirely on the third-party software agreements and plugin configurations set up by your specific educational institution's administration.
