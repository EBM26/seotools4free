How to make chatgpt write like a human?

To get ChatGPT to write in a more human-sounding way, you need to give it specific stylistic instructions rather than just asking it to write about a topic. Start by explicitly requesting a conversational tone, and ask it to vary sentence length naturally, mixing short direct sentences with longer, more detailed ones, since AI left unguided tends to produce uniformly structured paragraphs that feel monotonous. Instruct it to use contractions like 'don't' and 'you're,' include occasional personal-sounding phrases or rhetorical questions, and avoid overly formal transition words like 'furthermore' or 'in conclusion' that rarely appear in natural human speech or casual writing. Giving ChatGPT a sample of your own past writing, or asking it to mimic the style of a specific author or publication, gives it something concrete to model rather than relying on vague stylistic descriptions. It also helps to ask it to avoid starting every paragraph the same way, avoid excessive hedging language, and skip overly balanced 'on one hand, on the other hand' structures that read as artificially neutral. Finally, always review and personally edit the output afterward, adding your own specific examples, opinions, or anecdotes, since that final human touch is often what makes the biggest difference.
