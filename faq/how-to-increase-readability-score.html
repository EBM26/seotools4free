How to increase readability score?

Improving your readability score comes down to making your writing easier to scan and understand quickly, which matters both for actual human readers and for SEO, since Google tends to favor content that clearly satisfies user intent efficiently. Start by shortening your sentences and paragraphs, since long, dense blocks of text are harder to read both on screen and especially on mobile devices, so aim for sentences under roughly 20 words on average and paragraphs of just two to four sentences. Using simpler, more common vocabulary instead of unnecessarily complex or technical words also significantly improves readability scores, since tools like the Flesch-Kincaid readability test specifically measure sentence length and word complexity to generate their scores. Breaking up content with subheadings, bullet points, and numbered lists makes text far more scannable, allowing readers to quickly find the information they're looking for without wading through dense paragraphs. Using transition words like 'however,' 'because,' or 'for example' helps readers follow your logic more smoothly, which readability tools often reward as well. Tools like Hemingway Editor or the readability checker built into Yoast SEO can give you real-time feedback and specific suggestions as you write, highlighting overly complex sentences so you can simplify them before publishing.
