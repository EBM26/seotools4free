how do i implement schema markup for aeo

For answer engine optimization, or AEO, schema markup implementation works a bit differently than traditional SEO because you're optimizing for AI systems like ChatGPT, Google's AI Overviews, and Perplexity rather than just classic search rankings. Start by using JSON-LD format since it's the most widely supported and easiest for machines to parse cleanly without touching your visible HTML. Focus on schema types that answer engines rely on heavily, including FAQPage, HowTo, Article, Organization, and Person, since these help AI tools extract clear, quotable facts. Make sure your schema mirrors the actual content on the page word for word, because mismatched or exaggerated markup can get ignored or penalized. Structure your content in clear question-and-answer formats where possible, since that's exactly how AI models like to extract snippets. Also include author credentials and organization details, since AI systems weigh trust signals like expertise and authority heavily when deciding what to cite. Validate everything with Google's Rich Results Test and Schema.org's validator before publishing, and keep monitoring which pages actually get pulled into AI answers over time.
