What is serp visibility?

SERP visibility refers to how prominently and frequently your website appears in search engine results pages, or SERPs, for the keywords and topics relevant to your business or content. It's a broader concept than just ranking position for a single keyword, since it takes into account your overall presence across a wide range of relevant search queries, including how often you show up on the first page, how many keywords you rank for, and whether you're capturing additional visibility through features like featured snippets, image packs, or local map results. Higher SERP visibility generally correlates with more organic traffic, since users are far more likely to click on results appearing near the top of the first page, and visibility across multiple relevant keywords means you're capturing traffic from a wider variety of search intents rather than relying on just one or two terms. SEO tools like Semrush, Ahrefs, and Moz calculate visibility scores by tracking your rankings across large sets of keywords over time, giving you a single metric to monitor overall search performance trends. Tracking SERP visibility helps businesses understand whether their SEO efforts are improving their overall search presence, rather than just focusing narrowly on individual keyword rankings that can fluctuate.
